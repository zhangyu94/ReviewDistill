\section{Introduction}

TimerBias asks whether a visible kitchen timer changes how long home cooks simmer dry pasta.

\section{Study}

Forty cooks prepared one box of spaghetti. The timer-on group could see a countdown; the timer-off group used a wall clock.

As shown in the figure, simmer time dropped by two minutes when the timer was visible. We do not include a figure or a table in this manuscript.

Cooks in both groups used the same pot size. Water was always salted.

\section{Conclusion}

A visible timer is associated with shorter simmer times in this small sample.
