\section{Introduction}

PondCount estimates how many frogs are calling at a pond from a one-minute phone recording.

The pipeline has three stages: peak picking, clustering, and a count head. This paper proves that the count head recovers the true number of animals in every field tape we collected.

\section{Method}

We treat each chorus event as a 200\,ms window whose energy exceeds a threshold. Later we call the same unit a vocal bout and a call packet without stating that the three names refer to one span.

Peak picking follows the energy rule in the supplement. Clustering uses the same distance as prior work.

\section{Results}

On the hold-out pond, mean absolute error was 1.4 frogs. Field ponds had more peaks at dusk.

\section{Discussion}

The method is easy to run on a laptop. Future work will try birds.
